# Phase 6.2 implementation and ESDS validation

## What changed

The report now loads sourced current P/E, dated price, verified TTM EPS, and any sufficiently sampled historical P/E ranges automatically through `GET /api/companies/{symbol}/valuation/tested-pe`. The optional forward scenario form is collapsed by default. NSE supplies prices; future EPS growth, terminal P/E, and required return remain explicit forecasts or investor choices.

For newly listed companies, the report now shows how many dated prices can be paired with financial facts public on those dates. A short available-period P/E range appears once at least two comparable dates exist; it is labeled separately from the 1Y and 3Y ranges and never used as a supported forward valuation. The summary calls an unselected forward test "No scenario set" and identifies valuation confidence separately from results confidence.

When no valuation classification exists, valuation confidence is `NOT_ASSESSED`, shown as "Not assessed". `LOW` is reserved for a calculated scenario whose assumption and evidence quality remains limited. Older saved reports may still contain `LOW`; the frontend displays "Not assessed" whenever the verdict itself is insufficient.

Migration `0012_management_commentary` adds `management_statements` and `management_guidance`; `0013_commentary_documents` records period associations even when a document has no extractable statement. Each statement links to a company, financial period, event, document, page, publication date, attribution, type, and stable `S{id}` evidence identifier. The ingestion service scans processed official commentary PDFs associated by company, quarter, fiscal year, period end, and publication window. It rejects unattributed lines and does not convert management claims into verified financial facts. Transcript question and answer sections are marked when their text structure permits. Guidance is stored as successive dated observations; the service parses a stated FY percentage or crore range, compares revisions, and compares eventual annual actuals in Python. Explicit withdrawals remain attributed statements in the guidance-change history.

The deterministic results service uses verified comparable revenue and PAT growth as its base. Material operating deterioration, weak cash conversion, and margin contraction act as guardrails. A severe broad sequential decline without an established seasonal pattern caps an otherwise positive annual verdict at neutral. Thresholds are visible in the returned methodology. This is a result assessment, never a trade instruction.

`POST /api/companies/{symbol}/valuation/tested-pe` computes a conditional current P/E range from user-selected EPS growth, terminal P/E bounds, years, and required return. Formula: `terminal P/E × ((1 + EPS CAGR) / (1 + required return)) ^ years`. It also returns required terminal P/E and required EPS CAGR, exact inputs, rounded range, the relative valuation classification, and the selected assumptions with source and date. Default fair-range tolerance is 10%; an extreme gap is 35%. Both can be changed through `near_range_pct` and `extreme_gap_pct`. No universal absolute P/E threshold or hidden score is used. A price older than seven days leaves the current valuation verdict insufficient while retaining the dated scenario result. The same endpoint calculates historical 1Y and 3Y P/E summaries only when enough point-in-time observations exist; each historical date uses only then-public financial facts. Verified peer data has no current model, so that lens remains unavailable. Without explicit forward assumptions, the valuation verdict is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

The analyst prompt is V15. It receives bounded official statement excerpts with separate `S` IDs and must attribute commentary as a management claim. Qwen cannot set the deterministic result or valuation verdict. The investor report is assembled after model validation and stored with the analysis. The frontend displays verdicts first, commentary, guidance history, claim comparisons, editable P/E assumptions, and calculation details. Existing ratio cards retain their source drill-down.

## ESDS Q1 FY2027 consolidated audit, 29 September 2026

Local database migrations were applied through `0013`. The period is `financial_periods.id=4`, ending 30 June 2026, validated consolidated scope. The stored official [Q1 results PDF](https://www.esds.co.in/assets/images/investor-relations/Announcements/ESDS_BM_Outcome_BSE_NSE_Intimation.pdf) is `documents.id=1`, linked to event 1. Event 2 and document 2 are an **analysts/institutional investor meet invite**, not a transcript. The company [IR page](https://www.esds.co.in/investor) lists Q1 results and earnings-call audio; the existing processed PDF pipeline contains no Q1 transcript, investor presentation, or earnings release. Therefore this run discovered **zero attributable management statements, zero structured guidance, zero claim-versus-fact comparisons, and no commentary-resolved research gaps**. Audio is not treated as a transcript or as a quotable statement.

ESDS deterministic **results verdict: NEUTRAL, LOW confidence**. Revenue grew 7.280053% YoY (`M13`) and PAT grew 13.984816% YoY (`M14`). Revenue fell 20.204776% QoQ (`M16`) and PAT fell 56.739465% QoQ (`M17`). The available equivalent-quarter history does not establish whether those sequential declines are seasonal. Cash conversion is unavailable. EBITDA margin change is only +1.146834 basis points YoY (`M15`). The positive annual comparisons and severe unexplained sequential declines produce a mixed operating assessment; the verdict is generated by the general rule, not by an ESDS-specific branch.

At the dated manual 28 September price of **₹1,672.50** (`Q1`), verified diluted TTM EPS is **₹12.10** (`F75`, `F36`, `F61`; share-count check also uses `F77`, `F38`, `F64`). Calculated **P/E = 138.223140×**. The entered 163× external P/E remains a calculation cross-check whose basis is not verified. No EPS-growth and terminal-multiple assumptions were selected for the saved ESDS report, and there is not enough dated P/E history for a useful 1Y or 3Y range. **Tested P/E range: insufficient evidence. Valuation verdict: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, LOW confidence.** A user can submit explicit assumptions to test a conditional range; that does not turn those assumptions into verified forecasts.

**P/S audit:** sourced market capitalization ₹19,604 crore (`Q1`) ÷ verified TTM revenue ₹481.280 crore (`F65`, `F26`, `F52`) = **40.733045×**. The existing ~40.73× output is correct. **EBITDA margin audit:** prior comparable-quarter margin **43.017330%**, current margin **43.028798%**; change **+0.011468 percentage points = +1.146834 basis points** (`R_MARGIN`, `M15`, `M12`). Thus the prior 1.15 **bps** label is correct; 1.15 percentage points would be incorrect.

Open research gaps include an attributable Q1 call transcript or reviewed audio transcript; FY27 management guidance; causes of the sequential falls; equivalent-quarter seasonality; operating cash flow and cash conversion; debt/cash and working capital; a sourced forward EPS estimate; and enough date-consistent P/E history or verified quality-adjusted peers. No missing number or management explanation was filled in.
