12. **TTM:** four consecutive same-scope verified quarters, or FY annual + current Q1 − prior Q1 when all three are available. Revenue, PAT, EBITDA, EBIT, EPS, operating cash flow, capex and FCF are separately checked. EBITDA = PBT + finance costs + depreciation; FCF = operating cash flow − absolute capex. No annual/quarter overlap is double counted.
13. **Formulas:** P/E = price / reliable diluted TTM EPS; P/S = sourced market cap / TTM revenue; P/B = market cap / latest verified equity; EV = market cap + verified total debt − verified cash; EV/Sales = EV / TTM revenue; EV/EBITDA = EV / TTM EBITDA; earnings yield = TTM EPS / price × 100; FCF yield = TTM FCF / market cap × 100; forward PEG = calculated P/E / sourced forward EPS growth in percentage points.
14. **Zero/negative inputs:** zero or negative diluted EPS blocks ordinary P/E and scenario calculations. Nonpositive revenue/equity/EBITDA blocks the applicable multiple. Missing debt/cash blocks EV rather than assuming zero. Negative FCF remains a valid negative yield when inputs exist.
15. **PEG provenance:** a forward growth entry needs a URL and source type. Manual entry defaults to `USER_ASSUMPTION`; no Qwen invented forecast enters the calculation. PEG remains unavailable without positive sourced growth.
16. **Point in time:** financial periods are selected by publication time relative to the dated market close (15:30 IST). Date-only publication becomes eligible the next trading date. The metric detail retains period IDs, fact IDs, method, market date and scope.
17. **Lookahead:** `test_future_published_annual_facts_never_enter_june_valuation` publishes FY data on 1 July and values the company on 15 June. It checks the June PAT and confirms no future fact or period ID appears in any calculation input. The earlier `test_point_in_time_excludes_july_results_on_june_15` also passes.
18. **Valuation change:** `GET /valuation/change` compares old/new dated price, EPS and P/E. It returns price growth, earnings growth and multiple change from Python, requiring both reliable positive EPS and P/E. Unadjusted stock split/bonus history can distort price returns; the UI warns.
