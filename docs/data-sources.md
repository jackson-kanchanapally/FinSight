The ESDS IR page also lists annual reports, financial information, credit ratings, and prospectus material. Those sections have inconsistent or absent publication dates. This phase does not infer dates from a fiscal-year label or cross a prospectus disclaimer, so they are outside the initial live adapter.

The page displays `INE0DRI0102` as an ISIN, but this is 11 characters while the existing company schema requires 12. Its accuracy could not be resolved from a primary source during this review. FinSight does not store or match on that value; it uses the scrip code and symbol printed in the issuer's PDF.

## Current use and provenance

`LIVE`, `FIXTURE`, and `MANUAL` are stored origins. Fixture mode remains the safe default and is visually labeled. Live mode automatically covers registered NSE companies through the official symbol directory; the ESDS IR adapter still requires its own mapping. A source reference retains its original URL and raw source fields, including the date basis. The PDF bytes, SHA-256, download time, and numbered text pages remain linked to the event. A missing date or a blocked/changed source is reported as a source failure or skipped item, never silently substituted with fixture data.

NSE's RSS feed is an official public link, but the format is not a contractual API. BSE automation remains unverified. ESDS IR is a company page and can change without notice. A scheduled check every 30 minutes keeps request volume low; bootstrap is limited to a short window and is intended for local administration.
