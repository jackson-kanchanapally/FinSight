# Code of Conduct

Almonds welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces managed by the repository maintainers, as well as interactions where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and clear. Critique the code or proposal and explain your reasoning.
- Assume people may have different experience or communication styles. Help newcomers find the relevant context.
- Respect privacy. Share synthetic or redacted examples instead of another person's credentials, portfolio, or personal details.
- Accept feedback and corrections. Keep technical disagreements focused on evidence and the project's needs.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Repeated personal attacks or pressure directed at contributors and maintainers are also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately through a contact route published on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jackson-kanchanapally). If no private route is available, ask a maintainer for one without posting names, personal information, or incident details publicly. For a software vulnerability, follow the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Describe what happened, where it occurred, and any relevant context. Share only the information needed to review the concern. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, avoid unnecessary disclosure of the reporter's identity, and step back when they have a conflict of interest.

Maintainers may request a correction, remove content, restrict participation temporarily, or exclude a participant, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior. They should explain the reason to the affected participant when practical. Reports and requests for reconsideration can use the same private route; this project does not promise a particular response time.
